01. Introduction

Welcome to Monakotas, an application operated by Mon Reve Properties LLC ("Company," "we," "our," or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal data you share with us.

02. Information We Collect

We may collect information about you in a variety of ways depending on how you interact with our Services:

A. Personal Information You Provide Directly

When you register for an account, fill out forms, or communicate with us, we may collect personal details including, but not limited to: your name, email address (e.g., account contact), phone number, billing information, and any correspondence you send to us.

B. Automatically Collected Usage Data

When you access Monakotas, our servers automatically record information that your device sends. This data may include your Internet Protocol (IP) address, device type, operating system, unique device identifiers, browser type, pages viewed, time spent on pages, and crash reporting logs.

We use the information we collect for business and commercial purposes, including to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain Monakotas functionality.
  • Process transactions and manage your account settings.
  • Send administrative updates, security alerts, and support messages.
  • Analyze usage trends to improve application performance and UI/UX.
  • Detect, prevent, and address technical issues or illegal activity.
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms.

04. Information Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.
